What the GaPSC can do

The sanctions available under the Code, from lightest to heaviest. Suspensions and revocations are also reported to the national NASDTEC Clearinghouse, so they follow an educator to other states.

WarningTells the educator the conduct was unethical; more will bring harsher action.
ReprimandA formal admonishment on the record.
MonitoringQuarterly checks with the educator and employer for a set period.
SuspensionCertificate invalid for a set time. Breach of contract is typically 90 contract days.
RevocationPermanent. A voluntary surrender counts the same.
DenialAn applicant, including a teacher candidate, is refused certification.
